I'm Rupesh, a full-stack software developer based in Chennai, India. Since
September 2024 I've worked at [Testpress](https://testpress.in) on a learning
platform used by education businesses.

Day to day that means Python and Django, PostgreSQL, and Celery, with the
frontend work a feature needs. I like the parts of a system that are easy to
get wrong: real-time media, payments, background jobs, and anything involving
other people's APIs.

I'm early in my career and I'm fine saying that. What I care about is getting
better at the work itself — reading the code, understanding the failure, and
writing it down afterwards.
